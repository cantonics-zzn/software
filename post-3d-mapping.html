Advanced 3D Mapping Solutions Now Available

Technology 3D Mapping Projection

Cantonics-tech is pleased to announce the availability of our advanced 3D Mapping solutions. This technology enables precise projection mapping onto complex 3D surfaces, opening up new possibilities for creative visual displays.

3D Mapping Technology

Playing the 3D model file in Wavefront obj format, which can be exported by most of the popular 3D modeling software, such as 3DMax or Blender. The projection screen is some structural building or 3D printing models with the same size of 3D model files.

Supports 3ds Max, Blender exported standard OBJ 3D models, with precise 3D curved surface projection mapping.

Key Features

  • OBJ Model Support: Supports standard OBJ format from 3ds Max, Blender, and other 3D software
  • UV Mapping: Advanced UV mapping for precise texture projection onto 3D surfaces
  • Architectural Projection: Projection mapping onto building facades and complex structures
  • 3D Printing Support: Works with 3D printed models for precise projection mapping

Applications

This solution is widely used in:

  • Exhibition & Museum: Interactive exhibits and immersive museum displays
  • Cultural Tourism: Building projection shows, outdoor light festivals
  • Retail & Branding: Product launches, brand activation events
  • Events & Concerts: Stage visuals, concert projections
