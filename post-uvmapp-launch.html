Introducing uvMapp: Next-gen Auto Geometry Correction

July 25, 2024 �� Admin

Product Launch Technology Projection

Cantonics-tech is excited to announce the launch of uvMapp, our revolutionary camera-based auto geometry correction technology. This innovative solution transforms how projection systems are calibrated and aligned.

What is uvMapp?

uvMapp is a Camera based technique, for mapping the stage(multiple projection screens) coordinates into the scene(what we see) coordinates. With this technique, auto geometry registration and picture blending can be helpful to our technical staffs, no more handful work is needed.

uvMapp based on camera imaging technology, once obtains the coordinate relationship between the stage plane and the projection optical path, automatically performs image alignment and image blending, without too much manual grid fine-tuning, greatly reducing on-site debugging workload.

Key Features

  • Camera-based Mapping: Uses camera imaging technology to capture screen coordinates automatically
  • Auto Geometry Registration: Automatically aligns multiple projection screens without manual adjustment
  • Picture Blending: Seamless edge blending for smooth multi-projector displays
  • Rapid Deployment: Significantly reduces on-site setup time

Industry Applications

uvMapp is ideal for:

  • Exhibition & Museum: Museum, exhibition hall, government digital showroom
  • Cultural Tourism: Outdoor shows, building projection mapping, theme parks
  • Simulation: Interactive VR, flight simulation, driving simulation
  • Education: IT classrooms, lecture halls, campus TV broadcasting

"uvMapp represents a significant leap forward in projection technology," said the Cantonics-tech team. "We've developed proprietary algorithms that eliminate the tedious manual calibration process, making multi-projector systems more accessible than ever."
