About Cantonics-tech

Professional projection display control technology developer

Company Overview

Cantonics-tech is a professional high-tech R&D company specializing in projection display control technology. We maintain close cooperation with well-known enterprises in the industry, dedicated to creating multi-scenario display control series products.

Compared with traditional display control products, our company has developed proprietary automatic geometry correction and 3D mapping core algorithms, forming a complete independent technical system. We continue to deeply engage in image algorithm R&D, providing stable and reliable display control underlying technical support for various scenarios including exhibition halls, cultural tourism, simulation, and education.

Cantonics is a dynamic and innovative company, who is focused on developing display control technique, serving many professional companies. To overcome the difficulty induced by non-standard rectangle screen and projector/display alignment, we developed some creative geometry registration picture edge-blending tools, for we always understand, basic techniques are the power of innovation.
