Cantonics-tech is expanding its cultural tourism solutions with outdoor projection shows for theme parks and cultural attractions worldwide.

Cultural Tourism Applications

Outdoor image and light show, building projection mapping, theme park, theme paradise. Cantonics-tech provides comprehensive display control solutions for cultural tourism applications.

Key Solutions

  • Outdoor Projection: Large-scale outdoor projection displays for night shows
  • Building Mapping: Architectural projection mapping on building facades
  • Theme Parks: Integrated display systems for theme park attractions
  • Light Festivals: Coordinated light and projection installations

Case Study

One of our recent projects involved a major theme park where we implemented a multi-projector display system for their nightly water show. The system featured:

  • Multiple projectors with auto geometry correction
  • 3D mapping onto water screens and building surfaces
  • Synchronized lighting and projection effects
  • Remote monitoring and control capabilities

The result was a stunning visual experience that increased visitor satisfaction and extended park operating hours.
