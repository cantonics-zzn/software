uvMapp Auto Geometry Correction

Camera-based technique for automatic screen alignment

uvMapp is a Camera based technique, for mapping the stage(multiple projection screens) coordinates into the scene(what we see) coordinates.

Product Overview

uvMapp is a revolutionary camera-based technique that automatically maps the stage (multiple projection screens) coordinates into the scene (what we see) coordinates. With this technique, auto geometry registration and picture blending can be helpful to our technical staffs, no more handful work is needed.

This solution addresses the common pain point of manual geometric correction - a time-consuming and labor-intensive process that requires skilled technicians to painstakingly adjust each projection screen.

Core Features

Camera-based Mapping

Uses camera imaging technology to capture screen coordinates automatically.

Auto Geometry Registration

Automatically aligns multiple projection screens without manual adjustment.

Video or Windows Desktop Blending

Seamless edge blending for smooth multi-projector displays.
